Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Anna Goldfarb ("Anna", "I", "me") collects, uses and protects the personal data you submit through the contact form on this website (the "Site"), in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

In short: the information you submit is used only by Anna Goldfarb to respond to your inquiry and, where you go ahead, to provide the home organization or moving services you requested. It is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Anna Goldfarb, professional home organizer, operating in Portugal and internationally, is the data controller for the personal data collected through this Site. You can reach Anna directly using the contact details on the Site.

2. What data is collected

When you submit the contact form, the following information may be collected:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number / WhatsApp number (optional)
  • Location (city or country)
  • Service you are interested in
  • Any details you choose to share about your project or home

No data is collected through cookies or tracking scripts on this Site beyond what is strictly necessary to deliver the form submission.

3. Why your data is collected (purpose and legal basis)

Your data is used solely to:

  • respond to your inquiry;
  • discuss, plan and deliver the home organization or moving services you request;
  • keep basic records of client communication for as long as reasonably needed for the project.

The legal basis for this processing is your consent (given by ticking the consent box before submitting the form) and, once a project begins, the performance of the service agreement between you and Anna.

4. No third-party sharing

Your data is used exclusively by Anna Goldfarb to provide the services associated with your project. It is not sold, rented, traded, or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Your data may only be shared with trusted service providers strictly where needed to operate the Site and the form, such as:

  • FormSubmit, the third-party service that delivers form submissions by email; or
  • Netlify, the hosting provider for this Site,

both of which process data only on Anna's behalf and under their own privacy and security obligations, and never for their own marketing purposes. No other third party receives your data, and it is never transferred for commercial purposes.

5. How long your data is kept

Your data is kept only for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a client, for the duration of the project plus a reasonable period afterwards for record-keeping. If no project goes ahead, your inquiry details are deleted within a reasonable time once it's clear there is no ongoing need.

6. Your rights under the GDPR

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • restrict or object to the processing of your data;
  • request a copy of your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before you withdrew it.

To exercise any of these rights, simply contact Anna using the details provided on the Site. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

7. Data security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Children's privacy

This Site is not directed at children, and personal data is not knowingly collected from children.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, for example to reflect changes in how the Site or services operate. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please get in touch using the contact details on the Contact section of the Site.
